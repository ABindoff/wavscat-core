Webcam tapping: video pipeline against hand landmarks

Each frame goes to two analyses at once: the landmark-free tapvid pipeline (wasm) and MediaPipe Hands. Each hand's landmark trace then runs through the same resampling, cycle timing and features as the video component, so the two can be compared feature by feature. Nothing leaves this browser; only a 64 x 48 grid of each frame is kept, and the landmark coordinates.

Sit so both hands fit in the frame, palms towards the camera, about an arm's length away, in even light. Turn hand tracking off to record the video pipeline alone, for instance to see whether MediaPipe costs the camera frames; a video-only recording is kept alongside the one with landmarks. Hands are named as the person's own: their left hand appears on the right of the camera image, which this preview shows mirrored.
